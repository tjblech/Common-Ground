Designed to reduce friction

Critical support is often fragmented across many organizations, websites, and systems. Common Ground is intended to bring trusted resources into a structure that feels clearer, more searchable, and easier to navigate.

Trusted Resources

Highlight verified organizations and support services that users can rely on.

Clear Organization

Group information by category and need so people can find relevant help faster.

Accessible Design

Present information in a format that is easier to understand, compare, and act on.

Community-Focused

Connect direct services with broader community support, education, and outreach.

More than a list of links

The long-term goal is not just to collect resources, but to build a system that helps people identify what kind of support they need and navigate it with more confidence.
