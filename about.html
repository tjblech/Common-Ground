About

Built to make trusted support easier to navigate.

Common Ground brings legal, healthcare, and community resources into a clearer, more accessible structure so immigrant families can find help with more confidence.

Why Common Ground exists

Important support systems are often fragmented across many websites, organizations, and services. Legal aid, healthcare access, housing assistance, food support, and community resources may all exist, but they are not always easy to find, compare, or trust. Common Ground responds to that problem by organizing support in one place.

Clearer access

Instead of forcing users to piece together information from many disconnected sources, Common Ground organizes resources in a format that is easier to scan, understand, and use.

Trusted information

The platform is centered on reliable resources so people can spend less time sorting through uncertainty and more time finding relevant support.

More than a directory

Common Ground is designed around usability as much as information. The goal is not only to list resources, but to help users identify what kind of support they need, understand what each resource offers, and move through the process with more clarity.
